# Haar Atelier Product Context

## Register

brand

## Product Purpose

Haar Atelier Alkmaar is a premium local salon website with a small boutique webshop for Natulique haircare products. The site should make visitors feel confident booking an appointment while also making it easy to discover and buy products.

## Primary Users

- Existing salon clients who want contact details, prices, or products.
- New visitors deciding whether Haar Atelier feels like the right salon.
- Customers buying Natulique products after salon advice or discovery.
- The salon owner/team, who need the site to feel current, beautiful, and easy to maintain.

## Brand Personality

- calm;
- warm;
- refined;
- personal;
- natural/organic;
- editorial but not cold;
- premium local salon rather than generic ecommerce.

## Strategic Priorities

1. Make appointment booking/contact the primary conversion path.
2. Keep webshop access visible but secondary to salon trust on the homepage.
3. Use large photography and eventually motion/video to communicate atmosphere.
4. Make Natulique feel like an expert-recommended salon product line, not random shop inventory.
5. Keep the experience mobile-first, because owner references and likely customer browsing are mobile-heavy.

## Owner Taste Signals

Marloes likes:

- moving images;
- large photos;
- the colors and typography from Allure The Salon;
- an airy beauty/salon editorial feel.

Reference:

```text
https://www.allurethesalon.com.au/
```

Borrow from the reference:

- large editorial serif headlines;
- warm terracotta / clay color accents;
- large image/video hero;
- strong appointment and shop CTAs;
- mobile-first composition.

Do not copy:

- Allure identity;
- exact wording;
- over-fashion direction that loses Haar's softer, local, personal feel.

## Current Constraints

- Production domain must stay stable: `https://haaratelier-alkmaar.nl`.
- Redesign previews use: `https://haar.manndigital.nl`.
- Stripe checkout/order/webhook flow must not be disturbed.
- Payload is live source of truth for products and related storefront data.
- Static content editability will be planned later; do not overbuild CMS architecture during visual exploration.

## Anti-References / Avoid

- Generic Shopify template look.
- Dense product-grid-first homepage.
- Cold black-and-white luxury with no warmth.
- Too many tiny content blocks.
- Big full-site redesigns before visual direction is approved.
- Heavy video/motion that hurts mobile performance.
